Privacy Policy

Arc Bot Archive uses Google account authorization to support private, owner-directed project work.

Google data we access

Depending on the task, connected tools can access Google Drive files and their details, Docs and Sheets content, Gmail message headers and snippets, Calendar event details, Contacts, Analytics and Search Console data. Some permissions allow connected features to create or update records or send email. The document-reading connection also uses Google account identity information to confirm it is connected to the approved account.

How we use and share it

We use this information to retrieve records, maintain archives, prepare handoffs and perform tasks the owner directs. Workspace tools can retrieve Drive content, Gmail headers and snippets, and Calendar event details. An approved document-reading tool can return Google Docs text and related details to the AI agent that requested it. Information from Google may be processed by AI services the owner authorizes for the task. Retention periods for all providers and configurations have yet to be verified.

Archives and retained copies

Eligible material may be filed in Google Drive. Arc can read back filed material to verify it and retain signed receipts locally. Local archives, backups and copies retained by authorized tools or providers may have separate retention and deletion procedures.

Managing access and copies

The account owner can remove the app's access from Google Account connections. Revoking authorization does not delete copies already retained. The owner can review retained project records and use the applicable deletion procedures for each location or provider.

Public website and contact

This informational site has no registration, sign-in forms or site analytics. Its hosting provider may process basic request data to deliver and protect the pages. Direct privacy questions to the support contact on the Google authorization screen.
